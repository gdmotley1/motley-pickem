Fixing the record book

The text. Under each pin the record's name is 8.5px grey capitals and the holder is 10px grey, so what actually reads is a number and a name. You hold seven of the twelve records, which is why it looks like the section just says Grant.

The organization. Every group has four records in rows of three, so every group ends on one pin alone. And the two records nobody wants, fewest points in a week and worst pick, sit in among the achievements.

Every phone below is the app's real stylesheet and real pin markup, on the real season as the database holds it this afternoon.

Also broken, and fixed in every option

An unfinished week counts as a finished one. Week 2 is one game in, and that one game is being treated as the whole week:

  • The form chart dives from about 180 to nearly zero for all four of you.
  • The book says you hold Fewest points in a week: 0. It should be Nicole, 147, from Week 1.
  • It says Nicole has already won Week 2, so Most weeks won is shared. It should be yours alone.

The fix: a week counts once all twenty games are graded, or once the next week has started. Standings keep counting points as games finish, like now. The form chart only appears once there are two finished weeks to draw, instead of a heading over "nothing to plot yet."
