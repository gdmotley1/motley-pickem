## 8-bit arcade Board, for a theme week

Grant, 2026-09-12, picking the Jumbotron for the Board tab off a board of six: "save 2 as an
idea for another week just for fun, super awesome as well take a bow."

A 1989 football cartridge on a curved CRT: Press Start 2P, scanlines and screen glow, pixel
windows with a white frame inside a blue one, team logos shrunk to 18px sprites and scaled
up with hard pixels, a pixel football on the title, standings with a life bar of points
against the most each player can still finish on, blinking FINAL and live tags, and PRESS
START at the bottom.

- Mockup: `scripts/boardtab_arcade.py` and `scripts/boardtab_arcade.css`, rendered by
  `python scripts/build_boardtab_board.py` into `outputs/boardtab-board.html` (direction 2).
- Board artifact: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/4a7c0109-62f3-4961-b6e1-dbe00e33ad26
- A theme week would be a skin on the Board, the way `data-skin` already switches the Week
  and Board tabs, and could be turned on for one week from Setup.
